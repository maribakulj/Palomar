# Roadmap 2026–2027

*2026-11-09, after the first cycle (L1–L5) of the review of 2026-11-09.
This replaces `docs/roadmap-v1.md` as the plan; that file stays as the
record of what V1 set out to do.*

## Where Palomar stands

Palomar converts bibliographic, archival and museum descriptions through an
IR of assertions, keeps their provenance, reports what it loses, and lets a
curator decide what was only proposed. It is past the demonstration of an
architecture, and not yet qualified for an institution: a supervised pilot
on a real corpus is now in reach; unsupervised conversion is not.

## Three levels of maturity

Every behaviour is at one of three levels. A behaviour moves up only with
the evidence the next level asks for.

| Level | What it means | The evidence |
|---|---|---|
| **Experimental** | It runs and is tested on fixtures; its output may change. | Unit tests. |
| **Validated on a corpus** | Measured against an independent source, with its disagreements explained. | An evaluation in `docs/eval/` on a named or frozen corpus, rerun when a change concerns it. |
| **Qualified** | Accepted by an institution on its own profile, with its limits published. | A pilot's acceptance report. Nothing is here yet. |

## What is guaranteed, heuristic, experimental

- **Guaranteed** is what `docs/guarantees.md` lists: each line is a behaviour
  a named test pins, and is withdrawn if that test goes. Among them, since
  this cycle:
  - no path of a conversion names two of its input, output and manifest;
    the manifest names the bytes published (L1);
  - no constructor, rule or mapping gives a confidence by default, and a
    threshold never accepts an assertion that has none (L2);
  - the manifest lists the vocabularies, from a jar as from a checkout,
    each fingerprinted as the code reads it (L3);
  - a LIDO dimension says what it measures (L4).
- **Heuristic** is what follows words: roles, attribution qualifiers, event
  and subject types, the vocabularies under `resources/palomar/vocab`. Each
  table names its source. A word a table lacks names no concept there: the
  reader falls back (an actor type to a person, a qualifier to the roles, a
  subject type to *about*), and this is not reported yet. Some words are
  still code, not tables (ADR 0056, "What is still code"). Pending claims
  (`:proposed`, `:needs-review`) are heuristics by status: by default no fact
  target states them; `--with-proposals` does, and PROV-O, N-Quads, TriG and
  EDN keep them with their status.
- **Experimental** is the rest, notably:
  - the archival side (EAD, EAC-CPF, RiC-O): tested on toolkit and example
    files, no real corpus yet (ADR 0045);
  - performance beyond the corpora measured (`docs/eval/scale.md`).

## Validated on a corpus

- **Museum, LIDO → Linked Art and CIDOC CRM**, against the Yale Center for
  British Art's own Linked Art on 500 frozen pairs (`docs/eval/ycba.md`):
  makers, influences, materials, dimensions and the part they measure; that
  a custodian is given (the ids differ); subjects of the visual item in part
  (5 385 of YCBA's 6 089 `represents` labels since N7: mostly the concepts
  that no authority names are not written). This is agreement between two independent mappings,
  not correctness: neither is annotated truth. It is rerun by hand
  (`dev/eval/ycba`), not in CI: its data is fetched, never committed.
- **Bibliographic**, against independent golds committed with the tests and
  rerun in CI: Work clustering on BIB-R and entity resolution on
  OpenLibrary, on joinable subsets (`bibr-work-clustering.md`,
  `entity-resolution.md`); the MARC → DC and MARC → MODS crosswalks against
  the LoC stylesheets, on two records. The WEMI check (`wemi-fidelity.md`)
  is a transcription check (its gold and its input share one link), and
  `scale.md` a performance budget: neither is validation in this sense.

## Next: phase 3, the semantics

In this order, each lot with its measure before and after:

1. **Justification contracts.** What a rule requires of the source, what it
   asserts, and when it abstains, written with the rule, so that each derived
   assertion says what made it and what would have stopped it; and a word a
   vocabulary lacks, reported.
2. **Event and object classes as rules**, so that one place decides them
   (ADR 0056 says why they are still code). A property test holds the code
   and the rules to one event class until then; the object's class has no
   such test.
3. **Persons and places a work represents**, read like concepts: shown, or
   what the work is about. *Done in lot N7* (ADR 0056 amendment and its
   review): on YCBA, persons 0 → 125 `represents` and 6 `about`, as YCBA
   has them; places 0 → 726 `represents` against 737, one IRI written once
   (`docs/eval/ycba.md`).
4. **SHACL shapes** for the CIDOC CRM and Linked Art outputs, and
   consistency across serialisations: what is a proposal in one view is no
   fact in another, `--with-proposals` included.
5. **An annotated reference corpus**, to measure correctness where the YCBA
   comparison measures agreement: 150 records across the three domains, the
   undecidable kept apart, annotated twice by specialists. Palomar can
   prepare the schema, the guidelines and the selection; the annotation is
   people's work.

## Later

Incremental conversion (stable identities across versions, semantic
deltas, resumable OAI-PMH harvests), a distributable release with its
packaging tests, a pilot with institutions. Then, and only measured against
the benchmarks, assistance for open decisions (vocabulary alignments,
unrecognised qualifiers), new formats on a partner's need, and a review
interface.

## What is not planned

A general-purpose RDF store, a graphical mapping editor, an LLM that writes
triples, or new formats without a corpus and a partner.
